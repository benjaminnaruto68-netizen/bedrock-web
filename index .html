BEDROCK WEB

A little block world. Your adventure starts here.
Computer: click Play, then click the world to lock the mouse. WASD to move, Space to jump, left-click to break, right-click to place.
Phone/tablet: use the left pad to move, drag the screen to look, and use the action buttons.

Worlds are saved in this browser on this device. This starter version is single-player.
Adventure: explore without breaking or placing blocks.
Spectator: fly freely through blocks; building and breaking are disabled.
